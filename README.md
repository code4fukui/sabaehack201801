# sabaehack201801

> 日本語のREADMEはこちらです: [README.ja.md](README.ja.md)

障がい者に寄り添うオープンデータ活用ハッカソン

This repository contains projects developed at the "Hackathon for Utilizing Open Data to Support People with Disabilities" held in Sabae City.

## Projects

### 1. SKY VOICE (スカイボイス)
A navigation app concept that guides people with disabilities to their destination via real-time voice guidance from a global network of volunteers.

- **Demo:** [http://codeforfukui.github.io/sabaehack201801/skyvoice/](http://codeforfukui.github.io/sabaehack201801/skyvoice/)

#### Features
- **Voice Guidance:** Connects users with people around the world for live voice support.
- **GPS Matching:** Uses GPS to match the user with the most suitable guide for their chosen destination.
- **Price:** Free.

#### How to Use
1. Install the application on your smartphone.
2. Launch the app and tap your destination on the map.
3. The app matches you with an appropriate guide.
4. Follow the guide's voice instructions to reach your destination.

### 2. 鯖江市AIチャット「おたすけパンダPくん」 (Sabae City AI Chat "Otasuke Panda P-kun")
An AI chatbot, featuring the mascot "Otasuke Panda P-kun," designed to answer questions about Sabae City Hall.

- **Demo:** [http://codeforfukui.github.io/sabaehack201801/aichat/](http://codeforfukui.github.io/sabaehack201801/aichat/)

#### Features
- **Text & Voice Input:** Users can interact with the chatbot via text or voice.
- **AI Speaker Integration:** Can be linked with a dedicated, red panda-shaped AI speaker.
- **A Learning AI:** P-kun is a new AI that learns and improves as more people ask it questions.

#### How to Use
- Access the chat window on the Sabae City Hall website.
- Type or speak your question to P-kun to receive an answer.
